Data Downloads

Access comprehensive population data spanning more than a century. Available datasets include:

  • Total Population (1900 – 2020)
  • Population 18 Years of Age and over (2020 Census)
  • Total Population by Age and Sex (1960 – 2020)
  • Total Population by Race and Hispanic or Latino (2000 – 2020)
  • Population Change (2000 – 2020)

Population estimates provide essential demographic data between Decennial Census years. The Census Bureau, working with the Federal-State Cooperative Program for Population Estimates, produces annual estimates of total resident population for the nation, states, counties and incorporated cities.

Vintage 2022

Released in August 2022, the Vintage 2022 Projections of Population and Households forecast population change through 2050 in five-year intervals for Kentucky, Kentucky counties and Area Development Districts.

Population projections forecast future population using models that integrate recent historical data and standard demographic processes. The cohort component model used for these projections captures the impact of births, deaths and net migration to forecast population changes.

These projections used a bottom-up approach where county projections were generated first, then aggregated for Area Development Districts and the state. The 2020 Decennial Census and 2021 Population Estimates provided base population data.

Complete Report:

  • Population and Household Projections–Kentucky, Kentucky Counties, and Area Development Districts–2020-2050, Vintage 2022 

Analysis-ready files:

  • Population by Sex and Five Year Age Group (includes total population, population in households, and population in group quarters)
  • Codebook for Projections by Sex and Five Year Age Group
  • Population by Race and Ethnicity
  • Households
