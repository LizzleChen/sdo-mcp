Acknowledgements

The Missouri Population Projections - Preferred Series is the product of the hard work of many people. In particular, Ryan Burson, former Missouri State Demographer, did much of the work required to compute these population projections. Matt Hesser filled the position of Missouri State Demographer and worked with Mr. Burson to finalize the projections. Several iterations of the projections were reviewed by a dedicated group of volunteers familiar with both the process of calculating population projections and with demographic trends in Missouri. The Missouri Population Projections - Preferred Series is the culmination of the efforts of these individuals. The Office of Administration - Division of Budget and Planning would like to give special thanks to the following individuals who volunteered their time and valuable skills during the review of these population projections.

Office of Social and Economic Data Analysis
