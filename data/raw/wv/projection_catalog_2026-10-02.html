Data

The Bureau of Business and Economic Research (BBER) data website is dedicated to providing a one-stop access to a variety of data concerning West Virginia and its regions. Some data are already tabulated and directly accessible, others require users to follow queries, which should be self-explanatory. Depending on the subject, data are mostly available for West Virginia and its 55 counties, but in some cases are available for other states and metropolitan areas in the U.S. as well.
