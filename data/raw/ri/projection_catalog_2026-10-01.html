Welcome

The Division of Statewide Planning prepares and maintains plans for the physical, economic, and social development of the state; encourages their implementation; and coordinates the actions of state, local and federal agencies and private individuals within the framework of the state's development goals and policies.

Committee Participation Form and Information

If you are interested in becoming more involved in the Rhode Island’s state-level volunteer planning committees, we would love to hear from you! Please follow the link to read about the three principal committees of the State Planning Council, the Technical Committee, the Transportation Advisory Committee (TAC), and the RIGIS Executive Committee. While appointments for three-year rotating terms are typically made in the fall each year and begin in January, you can indicate your interest any time of year, and staff will be in touch.

Census Happenings

2030 Census

  • 2030 Local Update of Census Addresses (LUCA) Training Webinar: Wednesday, June 3, 2026 1:00 PM - 3:00 PM
  • The 2030 Census Local Update of Census Addresses (2030 Census LUCA) Operation is an opportunity for tribal, state, and local governments to review and comment on the Census Bureau's residential address list. 2030 Census LUCA is planned to start in 2027, however many governments may want to start preparing now. To help all governments plan for 2030 Census LUCA the Census Bureau has released the 2030 Census LUCA webpage with many operational details.
