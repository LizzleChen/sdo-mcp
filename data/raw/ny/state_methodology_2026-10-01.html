Methodology overview

These projections are based on a cohort-component method. This method is based on the demographic balancing equation:

Popt+1 = Popt + Births - Deaths + (In - Out)Domestic + (In - Out)International

The projection model starts with the population at time t, ages everyone by 1 year, and based on the composition of this population and a set of assumptions, projects each of the so-called components of change.
